# Third-party notices

As of 2026-10-06, MJobs does not intentionally include code, assets, prompts,
documentation text, or license text copied or adapted from the reference
projects listed in [`docs/reference/REFERENCE_PROJECTS.json`](docs/reference/REFERENCE_PROJECTS.json).
That registry records research provenance and allowed-use posture; it is not a
third-party distribution notice for MJobs dependencies.

Before any third-party reference material is intentionally incorporated, review
the exact source file and commit, compatibility, copyright and license notices,
NOTICE/patent obligations, required modification notices, and distribution scope.
Record the component, upstream URL/ref, license, required attribution, and
modifications here before merging. Do not use similarity or a current default
branch license to infer the terms of a historical copy.
